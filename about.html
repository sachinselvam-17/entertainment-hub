INTEGRITY & ETHICS

Our Creator Ethics & Copyright Policy

1. Strict Fair Use & Anti-Piracy: We respect filmmakers, athletes, and broadcasters. We never host, download, or redistribute live match footage, full film streams, or pirated content. All imagery used on this site is original or legally licensed.

2. Unbiased & Unbought Critiques: Our movie reviews and match analyses are 100% independent. We do not accept paid positive reviews or studio talking points. If a movie has a weak screenplay, we say it candidly; if a production hits a masterclass, we celebrate it wholeheartedly.

3. Respectful Fan Culture: Sports rivalry and cinema fandom are about passion and fun. We maintain a zero-tolerance policy for abuse, personal attacks, and hate speech in our community discussions.
